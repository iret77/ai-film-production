# Director Recipes: Combined Style Packages (Appendix)

Optional starting points, NOT a replacement for film-craft.md — each recipe combines composition, camera, editing, light, color, and timing into one coherent package a project can adopt and adapt. Legally these are aesthetic lineages ("in the spirit of, fully original execution" — the established formula, platforms-models ch. 13); never reproduce protected characters, titles-as-content, or scenes. Offer recipes when the user wants a fast style decision; mix at most TWO, with one dominant.

Format per recipe: Feels like → Composition · Camera · Editing · Light · Color · Timing · Sound cue · Best for · Verify. The **Verify line is the reroll gate**: binary checks to run against generated stills/takes before accepting a result as on-style — a shot that fails its Verify checks is off-recipe even if it looks good.

## Selection index: brief → recipe

Entry paths, in priority order:

1. **Genre named or evident** → `genre-baselines.md` carries the per-genre recipe shortlist; come back here for the full package + Verify line.
2. **Director/DoP named and listed** → use that recipe directly.
3. **Director/DoP named but NOT listed** → check the alias table below first; otherwise map to the nearest package on the eight dimensions and say so ("closest package here: X") — never silently substitute.
4. **Mood/feeling given** → mood table below; offer max 2 candidates with their "Feels like" line, the user picks.
5. **Brief too vague to map** → micro-interview, max 3 questions, one at a time: one-word feeling? · fast or slow? · real or stylized? — then mood table + constraint filter, offer 2. Never dump the recipe list on the user.
6. **No single recipe fits** ("something between X and Y") → synthesis: pick ONE base recipe, negotiate deviations dimension by dimension (the eight dimensions are the interview agenda), keep the base's Verify checks where undeviated. The two-package cap still applies.
7. **Always before recommending:** run the constraint filter, then pick the DoP pairing from the harmony map (documented partnership first).
8. **After the pick:** the chosen recipe (or synthesis) is written into the project's style-anchor paragraph (SKILL.md workflow step 4, reused verbatim in every prompt), and its Verify line joins the QA gate for stills and takes. Record recipe + anchor in the production bible (ch. 22). The recipe is the bridge from story dialogue into preproduction — not a mood board.

**Constraint filter (veto BEFORE recommending):**
| Project constraint | Favors | Penalizes |
|---|---|---|
| Low reroll budget / tight deadline | Malick (mosaic of short moments), Boyle (montage), Ozu (locked frontal statics), Gilligan (wordless process montage) | Tarkovsky, Lubezki-style oners — long coherent takes mean extension chains + high reroll cost |
| Complex action, crowds, physics risk | Greengrass, UGC, Lynch — absorb or legitimize artifacts; Kurosawa — telephoto crowd planes + weather cover | Fincher, Kubrick — surgical frames expose every flaw |
| Dialogue-heavy | Ozu (frontal singles sidestep reverse-angle continuity), Tarantino, Bergman | Greengrass — verité dialogue coverage burns takes |
| Film lives on face performance | Bergman + Nykvist — spend the PERFORMANCE/reroll budget here | Willis (buried eyes), heavy-silhouette styles |
| High continuity risk (many locations/outfits/characters) | Malick, Lynch — elliptical/dream logic tolerates jumps | Nolan cross-cutting — parallel lines punish continuity errors |
| Stylized/animated format | Miyazaki, Anderson, Pixar → pixar-look.md | Fraser/Willis deep underexposure — clashes with readable-face warmth |

If the mood pick and a constraint collide, state the trade in one sentence and let the user decide — never silently downgrade the style.

**Wes Anderson (symmetry & deadpan)** — Feels like a storybook diorama with dry wit. Composition: dead-center symmetry, planimetric (flat-on) tableaus, meticulous production design. Camera: locked-off or precise 90° whip pans, lateral tracking like page-turns. Editing: rhythmic, chapter cards, inserts of objects/hands. Light: soft, even, almost shadowless. Color: pastel palettes, one accent per world. Timing: metronomic; deadpan holds after punchlines. Sound: needle-drops, narrated formality. Best for: whimsy, families, melancholy comedy, stylized worlds. Verify: subject dead-center, flanks mirrored? camera exactly frontal, no perspective diagonals? light shadowless? ONE accent color against pastel? deadpan hold after the beat?

**ZAZ / Mel Brooks (parody deadpan)** — Feels like a serious film that has no idea it's ridiculous. The META-recipe: adopt the TARGET genre's package verbatim (cop procedural, disaster movie, western) and play it 100% straight — comedy arises from content against faithful form, never from a winking camera. Composition: the parodied genre's framing, plus gag layering — foreground action earnest, absurdity in background/midground planes. Camera: exactly what the target genre would do; no comedic moves. Editing: straight genre pacing; cutaways deliver punchlines deadpan. Light/Color: the target genre's package, unbroken. Timing: gag density over gag size — next joke before the laugh dies; rule of three, the fourth breaks it. Sound: earnest genre score played straight (the score never laughs). (Monty Python variant: the FORM itself breaks instead — intertitles, animation inserts, abrupt endings; anarchy of format, not of frame.) Best for: spoofs, genre parody, sketch features. (AI note: cheap and robust — reuse an existing recipe wholesale and inject gags as content; background-gag layers are still-friendly; deadpan needs the PERFORMANCE block to suppress smiles.) Verify: would a muted single frame pass as the SERIOUS genre? background gag present but not framed as the subject? no camera move that "knows" the joke? score earnest? faces dead straight?

**Woody Allen lineage (master-shot talk comedy)** — Feels like eavesdropping on smart people failing at life. Composition: warm book-lined interiors and city exteriors as conversation arenas; two-shots over singles; characters allowed to LEAVE frame while the dialogue continues. Camera: long-take masters, walk-and-talk lateral tracking, reframes instead of cuts; almost no coverage. Editing: scenes play whole in one or two setups; cuts between scenes, rarely inside them. Light: warm practical interiors, autumn-city naturalism (the Willis NY period: underexposed amber rooms). Color: amber/brown warmth, muted city palettes. Timing: overlapping neurotic dialogue tempo against calm static frames; the joke lands verbally, the frame stays polite. Sound: jazz/classical needle beds; direct address or essayistic VO legitimate. Best for: intellectual/dialogue comedy, relationship pieces, essayistic city films. (AI note: master-shot discipline = few setups per scene = cheap coverage; but long dialogue takes demand lip-sync quality and multi-beat PERFORMANCE writing — budget there; dialogue continuing OUT of frame is a legitimate lip-sync rescue.) Verify: scene holds in one or two setups? characters permitted out of frame mid-line? warm practical interior light? two-shot preferred over shot/reverse? no cut inside a joke?

**Edgar Wright (comedy in the cut)** — Feels like the film itself is telling the jokes. Composition: center-punched frames set up FOR the gag, symmetry as setup, inserts as punchlines. Camera: crash zooms, whip pans, snap push-ins timed to the beat — every move is a gag or a setup. Editing: THE instrument — match cuts, hyper-compressed montage (mundane tasks cut like action scenes), rhythmic repetition with escalation, scene transitions that are jokes. Light/Color: genre-bright saturated pop; pastiche lighting when the scene parodies a genre. Timing: everything ON the beat — needle-drops synced to cuts, gags on the downbeat, rule of three across scenes. Sound: effects as punchlines (every whoosh scored), diegetic music timed to action. Best for: action-comedy, genre-blend comedy, montage humor, music-driven set pieces. (AI note: the comedy lives in POST — generate clean coverage of each beat and build the timing in the edit; crash zooms/whips are cheap prompt moves; the mundane-task-as-action montage is generation-friendly — short independent clips.) Verify: cuts landing on the beat? at least one transition IS a joke? inserts fired as punchlines — fast, tight, loud? camera moves motivated by comedy, not coverage? escalating repetition present?

**David Fincher (surgical control)** — Feels like being watched by something patient. Composition: geometric, ruled lines, subject boxed by architecture. Camera: the "silent machine" — motivated micro-moves, no handheld tremor, camera height at subject logic. Editing: invisible, information-dense, cuts on cognition. Light: low-key, top-ish soft sources, shadowed eyes, greenish-yellow practicals. Color: desaturated teal/amber/sickly green. Timing: relentless even pulse, no wasted frame. Sound: low ambience beds. Best for: thrillers, procedurals, tech dread. Verify: zero handheld tremor, every move slow + motivated? eyes shadowed by top-ish key? subject boxed by architecture lines? teal/amber/sickly-green grade, no clean whites? every cut lands new information?

**Roger Deakins lineage (DoP grammar, Mendes class)** — Feels like monumental clarity. Composition: vast negative space, tiny humans in geometry, silhouettes against gradients. Camera: slow disciplined moves, long holds, one idea per shot. Editing: patient, cuts as chapter beats. Light: single motivated source pushed to its limit (fire, window slab, sodium). Color: constrained near-monochrome fields (dust orange, fog gray, night blue). Timing: dread through duration. Sound: bass swells, silence weaponized. Best for: epic sci-fi, war, existential scale. Verify: ONE readable idea per frame? human tiny against geometry or silhouetted against a gradient? single motivated source, no fill from nowhere? near-monochrome color field? shot held long enough to feel duration?

**Stanley Kubrick (one-point dread)** — Feels like fate has a floor plan. Composition: one-point perspective corridors, ruthless symmetry. Camera: slow dolly/steadicam glides, wide lenses close to faces. Editing: long takes, then brutal cuts; classical music against horror. Light: motivated-but-exaggerated (candles, fluorescents), often flat-wide. Color: era-saturated, single-color rooms as psychology. Timing: glacial escalation. Best for: horror, hubris, institutions. Verify: one-point perspective, vanishing point centered? flanks mirror each other? glide perfectly steady, no wobble? room owned by one dominant color? escalation glacial, no early cut?

**Steven Spielberg (invisible blockbuster grammar)** — Feels like being told a story by a master who never shows his hands. Composition: depth-staged frames (action on three planes at once), faces framed against wonder or threat. Camera: the "oner" — long takes where BLOCKING creates new framings instead of cuts (actors and camera reposition into fresh compositions); slow push-in on a realizing face (the "Spielberg face"); low angles at child eye-height for awe. Editing: invisible continuity, reaction before reveal — we see the face FIRST, then what it sees (builds anticipation); geography always clear. Light: motivated god-rays, backlit door/silhouette entrances, warm practicals against cool night. Color: naturalistic warmth, amber nostalgia vs. steel threat. Timing: suspense by withholding the full view of the threat (show pieces, delay the whole); sentiment earned through delay, then held. Sound: score carries emotion openly, silence before impact. Best for: adventure, family wonder, awe-and-threat storytelling, anything that must feel effortlessly classical. Verify: three depth planes active? reaction face BEFORE the reveal? god-rays/backlight motivated by an in-world source? threat shown only in pieces? geography never lost across cuts?

**Denis Villeneuve (slow monumental dread)** — Feels like standing before something vast that does not care about you. Composition: extreme scale contrast (tiny human vs. megastructure/landscape), silhouettes against gradient voids, brutalist geometry, fog/dust as depth. Camera: glacial dolly and crane moves with absolute discipline, long approach shots that let dread accumulate, aerials as chapter punctuation. Editing: very long takes, cuts as slow heartbeats; information released in measured drops. Light: single massive motivated sources (a slab of window light, a dust-orange sky), deep underexposure trusted to hold. Color: near-monochrome atmospheric fields per sequence (ochre, slate, pale gray), desaturated humanity inside them. Timing: tension through DURATION and approach, not cutting; the pause before contact is the scene. Sound: sub-bass drones and BRAAM-class hits fused with score, silence as pressure. Best for: sci-fi of scale, first contact, institutional dread, existential thrillers. (Shares the Deakins DoP grammar above; Villeneuve adds the slower macro-rhythm, scale obsession, and sound-design fusion.) Verify: scale contrast extreme — tiny human vs. mass? atmosphere (fog/dust) building depth? one massive motivated source? one near-monochrome field per sequence? tension carried by approach/duration, not cut rate?

**Ridley Scott (layered atmosphere)** — Feels like a world so dense you could breathe it. Composition: deep frames layered with foreground obstruction — smoke, rain, fans, grids, silhouettes; monumental establishing shots; figures INSIDE environments, never in front of them. Camera: classical moves through volumetric space, slow reveals of scale, hard cuts to detail. Editing: patient world-building punctuated by violence; parallel tension lines. Light: shafts through smoke as signature (blinds, searchlights, god-rays), backlit atmosphere over clean key, practicals multiplied (candles, monitors, neon). Color: world-defining palette per project (amber candle-gold, steel blue-rain, desert bleach), always atmosphere-thickened. Timing: dread accumulates through environment; setpieces erupt from ambience. Sound: environment as score — rain, hum, machinery, wind chimes. Best for: sci-fi worldbuilding, creature dread, historical epics, any project where the WORLD is the star. (AI note: atmosphere is the free lunch — smoke/rain/backlight are prompt-cheap, hide artifacts, and read as production value; layered foreground occlusion breaks the sterile-AI-frame look; his ad-craft polish makes this the bridge recipe for commercials.) Verify: at least two atmosphere layers between lens and subject? light arriving as visible shafts/backlit volume? establishing monumental, figure inside the world? palette world-defining and held? environment audible?

**Danny Boyle (kinetic euphoria)** — Feels like adrenaline with a heartbeat. Composition: canted and wide-angle-close frames, subjects pinned to frame edges, bold graphic crops. Camera: restless energy — snap zooms, whip pans, low wide lenses running WITH characters; digital/available-light textures embraced. Editing: fast rhythmic montage cut to music, freeze-frames with inserted titles/graphics, time-lapse and speed shifts, fantasy inserts crashing into reality. Light: hard saturated practicals (sodium, neon, fluorescent), unashamed mixed color temperatures. Color: hyper-saturated pop palettes — acid greens, oranges, cyans. Timing: sprint-and-drop — euphoric acceleration, then a sudden held beat of stillness or horror; needle-drop as structural device. Sound: driving contemporary tracks fused to cut rhythm. Best for: urban energy, addiction/obsession stories, survival against the clock, montage-driven arcs. Verify: wide-angle-close distortion present? mixed color temperatures hot and saturated? cut rhythm locked to the track? at least one freeze/speed-shift/insert device? a held still beat after the sprint?

**Paul Greengrass (chaos verité)** — Feels like being inside the event. Composition: none held for long — found framings, crops, obstruction. Camera: handheld urgency, whip refocus, snap zooms. Editing: fast fragmentary, geography sacrificed for pulse (keep ONE orienting master per beat or the audience truly loses the room). Light: available/practical, unpolished. Color: newsprint desaturation. Timing: accelerando to impact. Best for: action, thrillers, docu-drama. (AI note: this style ABSORBS model artifacts — a legitimate rescue look.) Verify: framing genuinely found — crops, obstruction, late refocus? ONE orienting master per beat present? light available/unpolished? newsprint desaturation? no move reads choreographed?

**Dardenne / Arnold (social realism)** — Feels like life won't pause for the camera. Composition: tight following framings (the nape-of-the-neck shot), 4:3 or tight ratios boxing the subject, locations as found — no dressing glamour. Camera: handheld that BREATHES with the subject — following through doors, stairwells, streets in long unbroken takes; nothing a person couldn't carry, no crane, no drone. Editing: cuts only when attention shifts; scenes run past comfort into truth; no montage shortcuts. Light: available only — overcast daylight, institutional fluorescents, home practicals. Color: muted unglamorous naturalism, no grade beautification. Timing: real-time pressure; tasks and labor shown at true length; drama erupts mid-ordinary. Sound: location sound only, no score (or one diegetic song carrying everything). Best for: social drama, precarious lives, youth on the margins, moral pressure-cooker stories. (AI note: the anti-gloss is the hard part — models beautify by default: lock the anti-gloss POSITIVELY in the style anchor — "overcast available light, ungraded camera-native colour, unflattering fluorescents, skin as it is" (a repeated "no cinematic lighting" ban attracts it, video-prompting ch. 14 third class) — and lint outputs for accidental glamour; long following takes = plan extension chains.) Verify: camera plausibly human-carried, following not leading? zero beautification — light believable as found? location lived-in, undressed? scene runs past its "point"? no score present?

**Akira Kurosawa (motion and weather)** — Feels like history moving as one body. Composition: telephoto compression stacking crowds and armies into planes, triangular group blocking, frames alive in fore/mid/background at once. Camera: long-lens coverage of uninterrupted action, lateral tracking with runners and riders, static frames traversed by motion. Editing: cut ON movement to hide the cut, axial punch-ins, wipes as chapter turns. Light: hard sun and weather as co-director — rain, wind, dust, fog dramatized to the point of choreography. Color: B&W tonal richness or bold heraldic blocks (banners, costume as battlefield legend). Timing: stillness → eruption; group scenes breathe, battles land in waves. Sound: wind and weather beds, drums, silence before the charge. Best for: ensemble action, war epics, samurai/martial arts, any scene where motion itself is the spectacle. (AI note: weather is prompt-cheap production value AND hides artifacts; telephoto crowd planes tolerate face softness naturally; choreography must be written move-by-move.) Verify: motion layered in at least two depth planes? weather/element actively moving? telephoto compression — background stacked close? cuts landing ON movement? group blocking triangular and readable?

**Sergio Leone (opera of faces)** — Feels like myth stretched over minutes. Composition: extreme wides ↔ extreme close-ups (eyes), horizon staging. Camera: slow push-ins to duel framings, crane reveals. Editing: elastic time — long waiting, explosive resolution in seconds. Light: hard sun, dust, deep hat shadows. Color: bleached earth tones. Timing: the wait IS the scene; score and cut in lockstep. Best for: westerns, standoffs, revenge arcs. Verify: extreme wide ↔ eye-level extreme close-up alternation? hard sun, deep hat/face shadows? bleached earth palette? the wait held long before the burst? horizon staged as a line?

**Vince Gilligan (procedural corrosion)** — Feels like watching consequence assemble itself, one careful step at a time. Composition: neo-western wides (tiny figures against desert and sky), foreground-object dominance, the signature object-POV (camera inside/under the thing — barrel, safe, shovel, dryer); symmetry when institutions appear. Camera: locked wide masters held long, slow creeping push-ins, God's-eye overheads, time-lapse skies as chapter punctuation. Editing: cryptic cold opens (flash-forward fragments), wordless process montages cut to ironic needle-drops (the HOW is the scene), patient takes broken by sudden violence. Light: hard high-desert sun with deep face shadows, sodium-and-fluorescent interiors, silhouettes in doorways against blown exteriors; chiaroscuro deepens as the moral rot does. Color: character-coded wardrobe as arc state (the palette IS the character sheet), desert ochre against sky cyan, sickly institutional greens. Timing: slow-burn procedure — suspense from watching competence, dread from knowing the cost; long silence, short eruption. Sound: desert wind, amplified diegetic detail (clicks, hums, tools), needle-drops at ironic distance. Best for: slow-burn crime, transformation/corrosion arcs, procedure-as-suspense, neo-western Americana. (AI note: unusually AI-friendly — object-POVs are prompt-cheap distinctiveness with zero continuity cost, and wordless process montages fit generation limits perfectly: short independent clips, no lip-sync; but color-coded wardrobe demands the continuity ledger.) Verify: one object-POV or God's-eye per sequence? figures small against desert/sky wides? process steps wordless, each step readable? wardrobe color matching the character's arc state? hard sun shadows or sickly practicals — never neutral studio light?

**Wong Kar-wai (romantic blur)** — Feels like memory in a rainy neon city. Composition: frames within frames, mirrors-as-shape, bodies cropped. Camera: handheld intimacy, step-printed smear on motion. Editing: elliptical, repetition as longing, voiceover over time-jumps. Light: neon practicals, smoke, wet reflections. Color: saturated reds/greens/golds. Timing: languid loops, sudden accelerations. Sound: repeated musical motif as memory. Best for: romance, longing, city nocturnes. Verify: frame-within-frame or mirror shape? motion smeared (step-print feel)? neon practicals + wet reflections? red/green/gold saturation? a motif repeating?

**Céline Sciamma (the exchanged gaze)** — Feels like desire studied until it becomes mutual. Composition: painterly static frames — figures composed like canvases, faces given the time and centrality of landscapes; two people sharing frames as equals; interiors and nature pared to essentials. Camera: calm, frontal, unhurried; movement rare and meaningful (a turn, an approach); we look WITH, never AT. Editing: long takes; patient shot/reverse of gazes held past comfort into intimacy; ellipsis over exposition. Light: candlelight, firelight, window daylight, dusk — natural sources only, faces modelled softly. Color: restrained earth-and-sea palettes, one burning accent (a dress, a fire). Timing: slow reciprocity — every look answered; silence carries the negotiation. Sound: diegetic only, until music becomes an EVENT (never wallpaper — when it comes, it means). Best for: love stories, desire and consent arcs, period intimacy, quiet emancipation. (AI note: extremely still-friendly — static painterly frames are the stills-first pipeline's home turf; gaze shot/reverse needs the 180° lock and matched eyelines; music-as-event aligns with the no-generated-music doctrine.) Verify: frame would hold as a painting? gaze exchanged — both sides shot with equal weight? light honest from candle/window/dusk only? one accent color max? music absent until it's an event?

**Christopher Nolan (engineered time)** — Feels like a mechanism assembling. Composition: IMAX-scale clean wides, functional close-ups. Camera: mounted realism, aerial majesty, minimal ornament. Editing: cross-cut timelines converging (the structure IS the suspense), hard sound-bridge transitions. Light: naturalistic, cool. Color: steel blue/gray with warm human pockets. Timing: interleaved accelerando across parallel lines. Best for: heists, time mechanics, large-canvas thrillers. Verify: wides clean and monumental, no ornament moves? light naturalistic and cool? steel blue/gray with warm pockets only where humans are? each cross-cut line legible on its own? sound bridging the cuts?

**Wachowskis / Bill Pope (digital baroque)** — Feels like reality is a rendering you can bend. Composition: comic-panel decisiveness — poses as icons, low heroic angles, symmetrical confrontations, trench-coat silhouettes in sterile architecture. Camera: time-slice/bullet-time as signature (ultra-slowed moment orbited by the camera), wire-fu verticality, aggressive rack focus. Editing: action cut for choreography legibility (the Hong Kong debt); philosophical dialogue in formal shot/reverse. Light: green-tinted fluorescence for the constructed world vs. warmer truth — world-coding as dramaturgy; hard specular surfaces, rain and glass. Color: the green digital veil, black leather and chrome, desaturated real world. Timing: monumental pause before eruption; the held superhuman moment. Sound: impacts as sub-bass events, silence inside the frozen moment. Best for: cyberpunk, simulated realities, techno-noir action, transcendence arcs. (AI note: time-slice/speed-ramp is a documented model strength — write it as speed values, not the trademark term (video-prompting camera translation); world-color-coding is a cheap dramaturgical win; wire-fu = move-by-move choreography with hero-physics linting.) Verify: worlds color-coded and consistently held? max ONE time-slice moment per sequence, orbit readable? poses iconic — frame works as a comic panel? architecture sterile and specular? choreography legible through the cut?

**Hayao Miyazaki / Ghibli (animated humanism)** — Feels like wind you can trust. Composition: layered nature depth, characters small in living landscapes. Camera: calm pans, few cuts inside beats. Editing: "ma" — deliberate empty beats (riding, resting, weather) between plot beats; they ARE the film's breathing. Light: soft daylight, golden interiors. Color: watercolor greens/blues/creams (style-control §6 vocabulary). Timing: unhurried; action bursts read harder against calm. Best for: family, nature, quiet wonder. Verify: layered nature depth — fore/mid/background all alive? characters small in a living landscape? watercolor greens/blues/creams? "ma" beats present between plot beats? pans calm, no restless cutting?

**Pixar house style** — covered as its own sourced look bible: pixar-look.md (believability-not-realism, simplexity, saturation dramaturgy, Calahan light, curated lens imperfection).

**Quentin Tarantino (pulp tension)** — Feels like a conversation that might kill you. Composition: low "trunk shot" angles, table geographies, foot-level inserts. Camera: slow creeping push-ins during dialogue, sudden crash zooms. Editing: chapters, long dialogue takes, violence in abrupt spurts; needle-drop cuts. Light: warm practicals, 70s glow. Color: saturated pulp warmth. Timing: extreme delay before release (the Leone debt). Best for: crime, dialogue-driven tension, genre homage. Verify: low trunk-shot angle or table geography present? slow push-in during dialogue? warm 70s practical glow? violence abrupt and SHORT against long talk? chapter/needle-drop structure felt?

**Martin Scorsese (guilty momentum)** — Feels like being seduced by what will destroy you. Composition: subject pinned in a world too alive — busy frames, mirrors, neon interiors; the slow push-in on a face deciding. Camera: propulsive tracking oners through kitchens and clubs (the camera as insider), whip pans, crash zooms, God's-eye tableaus of aftermath. Editing: VO-driven momentum — freeze-frames, jump cuts, needle-drop hard cuts; pace as moral intoxication, then the long deflating comedown. Light: saturated practicals (bar red, club neon); violence erupts in ordinary flat light. Color: hot reds and ambers against urban grime. Timing: rush → stumble — sequences accelerate on the way up and hollow out on the way down; violence sudden, matter-of-fact, over. Sound: wall-to-wall needle-drops at ironic distance, VO confiding directly. Best for: rise-and-fall arcs, crime seduction, faith-and-guilt stories, unreliable charm. (AI note: the VO-plus-montage spine is generation-friendly — short clips stitched by narration, no lip-sync; the tracking-oner showpiece is the ONE expensive shot: plan it via the blockout path.) Verify: VO or music driving the cut rhythm? camera moving WITH the world's energy, not observing it? saturated practicals (red/amber) in frame? violence brief and unglamorous in ordinary light? one freeze/jump-cut device present?

**Alfred Hitchcock (pure suspense grammar)** — Feels like knowing too much. Composition: POV triads (look → seen → reaction), significant objects enlarged. Camera: dolly-in on realization, the vertigo shot for inner vertigo, god's-eye at moral distance. Editing: cross-cut bomb-under-the-table, cutaways that withhold. Light: hard noir keys on faces. Color: restrained with symbolic accents. Timing: audience ahead of character, always. Best for: thrillers, paranoia, one-location tension. Verify: look → seen → reaction triad complete? the significant object enlarged? dolly-in landing exactly on the realization? audience ahead of the character? hard key modelling the face?

**Wim Wenders (the seeing road)** — Feels like travelling with someone who notices everything and hurries nothing. Composition: landscape as co-protagonist — figures small against horizons, signage, gas stations; frames that hold after people leave them. Camera: patient observational pans and drives (through-the-windshield as worldview), lateral tracking past facades. Editing: unhurried, cuts follow attention not plot; scenes allowed to breathe past their "point". Light: real neon, dusk, motel practicals; color OR luminous B&W as a project-level decision. Color: Americana saturation (desert ochre, neon red, sky cyan) or silver-gray tenderness. Timing: journey rhythm — arrival, looking, moving on; dialogue sparse, listening long. Sound: diegetic radio/jukebox, slide-guitar loneliness. Best for: road movies, urban wandering, quiet redemption stories, documentary-fiction hybrids. Verify: landscape/signage co-starring — frame holds after people leave? light reads available, never studio? Americana saturation or luminous B&W held consistently? lateral tracking or windshield POV present? pacing follows attention, not plot?

**Andrei Tarkovsky (sculpting in time)** — Feels like a dream remembering you. Composition: painterly depth, ruins and nature interpenetrating, figures dwarfed by rooms and weather. Camera: very slow drifting takes that reframe meaning within one shot; imperceptible push-ins over minutes. Editing: minimal — the take IS the edit; time pressure inside the shot, not between shots. Light: natural gloom, windows as altars, candle/fire as fragile life. Color: desaturated earth and sepia, shifts to B&W or color as memory states. Elements as recurring matter: water dripping and pooling, wind through grass, fire, milk, mirrors-as-idea. Timing: duration as spiritual instrument — hold until discomfort becomes contemplation. Best for: memory, faith, grief, poetic sci-fi. (AI note: demands long coherent takes — plan extension chains and accept a high reroll budget.) Verify: take long with INTERNAL reframing — meaning shifts inside the shot? an element present (water/wind/fire/mirror)? natural gloom, window as altar? desaturated earth/sepia? push-in imperceptible?

**Yasujirō Ozu (domestic stillness)** — Feels like sitting with a family as seasons change. Composition: tatami-height camera (~90 cm), frontal symmetrical interiors, corridors and doorframes as frames, "pillow shots" — cutaways to objects/townscapes as breathing pauses. Camera: locked off, almost never moves; characters address nearly into the lens. Editing: 360°-space cutting (deliberately ignores the 180° rule — matched frontal singles instead), rhythmic regularity. Light: even, shadowless domestic daylight. Color: restrained interiors with one recurring red accent. Timing: ellipsis of the dramatic (deaths, weddings happen off-screen), attention on before and after. Best for: family drama, generational change, quiet heartbreak. (AI note: the frontal-singles system conveniently sidesteps reverse-angle continuity problems.) Verify: camera at tatami height (~90 cm), frontal? zero camera movement? dialogue in matched frontal singles, eyeline near-lens? pillow shots between scenes? the one red accent recurring?

**Ingmar Bergman (the face as landscape)** — Feels like being unable to look away from a soul. Composition: faces huge and centered, two faces overlapping in one frame (profile + frontal), stark figure-against-void. Camera: slow creep toward faces, long static holds on monologue. Editing: sparse; scenes as confessions played whole. Light: Nordic window softness against blackness, hard summer-island sun for exteriors; the face always modelled. Color: austere — chalk, black, blood red as eruption; or graphic B&W. Timing: silence between lines heavier than lines; dream inserts without warning. Best for: chamber drama, crisis of faith, marriages, illness. (AI note: lives on close-up performance — spend the acting/PERFORMANCE budget here.) Verify: face huge, centered, fully modelled? two-face overlap composition used somewhere? window-soft key against blackness? chalk/black austerity, red only as eruption? silence held between lines?

**Terrence Malick (whispered creation)** — Feels like memory praying. Composition: low sun through grass and hands, figures wading through nature, horizon-worship. Camera: wandering steadicam that circles and brushes past people, wide lenses close to skin, always moving gently. Editing: elliptical mosaic — moments not scenes, cut on emotional association; whispered voiceover stitches time. Light: magic hour as doctrine, backlit everything, no artificial feel. Color: golden wheat, deep green, sky pastel. Timing: drift; narrative dissolved into remembered fragments. Best for: memory pieces, nature-spiritual arcs, love and loss montage. (AI note: montage-of-moments structure fits generation limits unusually well — no long continuity chains.) Verify: magic-hour backlight, sun low and in or brushing frame? camera drifting/circling, never locked? cuts on emotional association, not scene logic? golden/green/pastel naturalism? whispered VO stitching the fragments?

**David Lynch (dream logic)** — Feels like the familiar turned inside out. Composition: ordinary Americana framed a beat too long and too centered; curtains, corridors, stages. Camera: slow floating dollies into darkness, sudden static wrongness. Editing: scene logic obeys dream association, not causality; inserts of objects charged with menace. Light: pools of light in black voids, flickering practicals, red lamps. Color: saturated red/blue against wood-panel browns. Timing: scenes held past comfort; sound arrives before its source. Sound: room-tone drones, industrial hum, sudden silence — the true horror instrument. Best for: surreal thriller, uncanny small towns, subconscious narratives. (AI note: dream logic legitimizes discontinuity — model inconsistencies can read as intention; still lock the recurring faces.) Verify: ordinary subject framed too centered, held too long? light pooling in black voids? red/blue saturation against browns? sound arriving before its source? recurring faces still locked despite the dream logic?

**UGC/found-footage (authorless camera)** — Feels like it really happened. The anti-recipe: composition accidental, camera device-logic (phone/bodycam/CCTV — one device per sequence, style-control §6b four-tell rule), editing in-camera cuts or none, light available-only, color device-processed, timing unshaped. Best for: horror, virality, testimony. (AI note: highest artifact tolerance of all styles.) Verify: ONE device logic held for the whole sequence (§6b four tells)? composition genuinely accidental? no grade beyond device processing? timing unshaped — in-camera cuts or none? nothing "cinematic" leaked in (no motivated key light, no smooth dolly)?


## DoP signatures (light/lens packages — combinable with any structure above)

DoP names are the established legal style currency ("References in spirit, fully original execution" — platforms-models ch. 13). Leaner format: pure image signatures without editing/dramaturgy; mix ONE DoP signature with ONE director structure. The Deakins package sits above as its own lineage recipe.

**Emmanuel Lubezki** — Natural light only, magic hour as religion; ultra-wide lenses inches from faces (distorted intimacy); flowing unbroken steadicam oners, camera as a body among bodies; sun flares hitting the lens as grace notes. Grade: naturalistic, luminous. Pairs with: Malick drift, survival epics, spiritual realism. Verify: light plausible as natural-only? wide lens inches from faces? flare motivated by in-frame sun?
**Hoyte van Hoytema** — Monumental cool naturalism; IMAX-scale sharpness with soft-window softness on faces; restrained palette (slate, sand, sea gray); practical-driven interiors, giant single sources; aerial majesty without gloss. Pairs with: Nolan mechanisms, cold-war textures, sober sci-fi. Verify: faces window-soft inside monumental sharpness? palette slate/sand/sea-gray? one giant source per interior?
**Greig Fraser** — Texture-first low-light digital: skin grain, dust, breath visible; underexposure trusted deep into shadow; silhouettes against atmospheric voids; anamorphic softness with brutal contrast pockets. Grade: monochromatic warmth or bone-dry desaturation. Pairs with: Villeneuve scale, noir superhero, candlelit intrigue. Verify: shadows deep but texture alive (grain/dust/breath)? silhouettes against atmosphere? contrast pockets brutal, rest soft?
**Vittorio Storaro** — Color as ideology: each act OWNS a hue (red passion, blue reason, gold decadence); painterly artificial light unashamed of theatricality; symmetrical torch-and-lantern compositions; moving light DURING the shot as dramaturgy. Pairs with: opera-scale drama, psychedelic war, myth. Verify: the act's OWNED hue dominating? light unashamedly theatrical? light moving DURING a shot somewhere?
**Gordon Willis** — The prince of darkness: top-light that buries eyes in shadow (moral unreadability); amber-and-black interiors, whole frames underexposed but composed; static formal wides, power geometry. Pairs with: crime dynasties, institutional evil, 70s paranoia. Verify: eyes actually unreadable in shadow? amber-and-black palette? frame underexposed yet shapes still composed?
**Janusz Kamiński** — Blown backlit highlights through smoke/dust; desaturated silver skin (bleach-bypass feel); handheld combat realism OR godlight sentiment — both extremes, nothing between; hard shafts through windows. Pairs with: Spielberg grammar, war memory, testimony. Verify: highlights blown through smoke/dust? skin desaturated silver? either handheld grit OR godlight — never a middle temperature?
**Christopher Doyle** — Handheld color fever: saturated neon smear, step-printed motion trails; cramped interiors shot through glass, rain, fabric; available fluorescents mixed hot; frames cropped by architecture. Pairs with: Wong Kar-wai longing, urban fever dreams, romantic disorientation. Verify: neon saturation with motion trails? shot through glass/rain/fabric somewhere? fluorescents mixed hot?
**Robby Müller** — Available light trusted completely: neon, dusk, fluorescents, motel and gas-station practicals as the ONLY sources; Americana color fields (desert ochre, neon red, sky cyan) or luminous silver-gray B&W as a project-level decision; unforced compositions that let landscape and signage speak; camera moves only when a vehicle moves. Grade: naturalistic saturation without gloss. Pairs with: Wenders road patience, Jarmusch-adjacent minimalism, urban nocturnes. Verify: every source visible/diegetic in the world? color fields clean, no gloss grade? frame calm, nothing decorative?
**Sven Nykvist** — The face light standard: soft window-side modelling, two faces one frame, nothing decorative; Nordic gray exteriors, candle-warm interiors; invisible technique in total service of performance. Pairs with: Bergman chamber work, intimate drama, illness and faith. Verify: face modelled from the window side, soft? nothing decorative in frame? warmth only from candles/interiors?
**Bradford Young** — Deep soft darkness with protected skin tones; underexposed frames that stay tender, not grim; practical pools in black rooms; haze halos around single sources. Grade: warm dark browns, muted jewel accents. Pairs with: quiet sci-fi, intimate histories, night interiors. Verify: darkness soft and warm, skin tones protected (not muddy)? practical pools in black rooms? haze halos around single sources?
**Claire Mathon** — Dusk and flame as primary sources: candlelight that actually models, blue-hour sea light, night trusted to real darkness; painterly stillness with digital cleanness (no nostalgic grain); skin luminous across tones; frames lit to hold as portraits. Grade: deep naturalism, velvet blacks, restrained saturation. Pairs with: Sciamma gaze work, period intimacy, sensual realism, night-sea poetry. Verify: source honest (flame/window/dusk) and actually modelling the face? blacks velvet, not crushed? frame holds as a portrait?


## Harmony map: which packages combine

Real-world partnerships are the proof that these pairings work — directors keep their DoPs because structure and image philosophy complete each other. Use the documented pairs as safe defaults, the cross-pairings as informed experiments, and respect the clash list.

**Documented partnerships (safe defaults):**
| Director structure | DoP signature | Why it harmonizes |
|---|---|---|
| Spielberg | Kamiński | Blown godlight + smoke gives the wonder-and-threat grammar its sentiment and its war-memory grit — both extremes in one toolkit |
| Nolan | Hoytema | Monumental sober naturalism scales to IMAX mechanisms without gloss; cool restraint matches engineered structure |
| Villeneuve | Deakins → Fraser | Deakins built the monumental-clarity phase; Fraser's texture-first underexposure carries the later dust-and-dread scale — both serve duration-dread |
| Wong Kar-wai | Doyle | Step-printed neon smear IS the longing; structure and image are inseparable here — adopt them together or not at all |
| Bergman | Nykvist | The face-as-landscape structure needs the face-light standard; nothing decorative survives the chamber |
| Allen lineage | Willis | The amber NY period — master-shot talk in underexposed warm rooms; comedy that trusts darkness |
| Sciamma | Mathon | The gaze structure needs flame-and-dusk honesty; portrait stillness is shared doctrine |
| Malick | Lubezki | Wandering whisper-montage requires a camera that lives in natural light and moves like breath |
| Wenders | Müller | Available-light Americana trust — the seeing-road structure needs a camera that treats neon and dusk as sufficient |
| Gilligan | (Michael Slovis / BCS lineage) | Hard-sun neo-western wides, object-POV wit, and time-lapse punctuation were built WITH the house camera department — the image grammar is part of the recipe; no full second package |
| Kubrick / Tarantino / Fincher / Scorsese / Ridley Scott / Wright / Wachowskis | (house signatures) | Their image grammar is baked into the director recipe itself — no external DoP package needed; adding one dilutes |

**Recommended cross-pairings (coherent experiments):**
- Hitchcock structure × Willis light — suspense grammar in moral darkness (paranoia thrillers).
- Leone timing × Storaro color — operatic waiting with ideological hues (myth, revenge).
- Boyle kinetics × Doyle fever — double adrenaline for urban delirium; keep ONE calm anchor scene or it exhausts.
- Ozu stillness × Nykvist faces — domestic chamber pieces of great tenderness.
- Lynch dream logic × Bradford Young darkness — soft uncanny instead of harsh horror.
- Tarkovsky duration × Fraser texture — modern low-light poetry (grief, faith, slow sci-fi).
- Greengrass verité × Kamiński combat realism — war/action documentary punch.
- Gilligan procedure × Fraser texture — late-arc corrosion in deep low-light (empire endgames).
- Kurosawa motion × Kamiński combat realism — weather-blown war epics with blown-highlight grit.
- Anderson symmetry × Storaro color — heightened storybook theatricality (dose it; two maximalists).

**Clashes (don't combine):**
- Lubezki natural-light flow × Fincher/Kubrick control — improvised light against surgical structure contradicts both.
- Storaro theatricality × Greengrass/UGC verité — ideological color dies in found-footage logic.
- Willis darkness × Miyazaki/Pixar family warmth — buried eyes against readable faces.
- Doyle smear × Nolan mechanism — motion fever blurs the machine's legibility.
- Scott atmosphere layering × Dardenne/Arnold anti-gloss — production value against found truth contradicts both.
- More than TWO packages total, ever — one structure + one signature, one dominant (film-craft ch. 9 logic).
