**Tag legend (canonical):**
- 🟢 verified — multi-source, official, or production-proven · 🟡 plausible — single-source or untested · 🔴 marketing claim — verify yourself · ⚠️ source conflict — the sentence next to the marker settles it (SKILL.md Precedence).
- [PP] first-party production-session evidence · [W] multi-source web research · [A] platform academy docs.
- **Source tiers — claims are not weighted one to one.** Tier 1: the model vendor's own documentation and first-party measurement [PP]. Tier 2: platform tutorials and established practitioners (YouTubers, bloggers, filmmakers). Tier 3: single finds in social posts, videos and sites — often the home of a specific trick, but in a frontal contradiction with a vendor statement they do not win without striking proof; this keeps engagement-farming know-how out of the skill. A contradiction is settled by tracing BOTH sides: a canon line worded too generally is narrowed; evidence on both sides makes both variants admissible, one per prompt; a canon line whose evidence cannot be found is outdated. Know-how for an older model version holds on the newer one until version-specific information says otherwise.
